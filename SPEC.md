# SPEC: my Sovran Advisor

What this app should do for its owner. It starts as Sovran Advisor's own description; the owner
(with their Claude) adds their own wishes here as the app grows with them.

## What it is for

A trusty companion on the Sovran Quest journey: one place for a journal, the people who matter,
notes, and an assistant that knows them, all kept on the owner's own computer.

## What it does from the start

1. **Journal.** One page per day, for writing: a few lines or a long entry.
2. **People.** Who someone is, how you met, notes about them, and a rhythm for staying in touch.
3. **Notes.** Clean pages with a title, linked to each other, searchable.
4. **The assistant.** The owner's own Claude, reading and writing their notes, journal and people
   when asked, and, with a library key, the Sovran teaching and their own decodes.

## Built in, switched on when wanted

Tasks, Planner, Calendar, My Life, My Life in depth (Habits), Google Calendar, Dictation and Mail (Settings › Modules). Mail waits for setup directions.

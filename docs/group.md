Group Circle is your Quest group's shared space. Daniel posts homework, reminders and news in **Announcements**, and everyone in your group shares in the **Circle**. Only your own group sees it: someone in another Quest never sees your posts, and you never see theirs.

**Watch the film:** [Group Circle in the Tour](/tour?film=group) shows all of this in about five minutes, alerts on your computer and phone included.

## How you use it

- Open **Group Circle** in the sidebar. A red number beside it says how many new posts wait. At the top of the page are two tabs, **Announcements** and **Circle**, each with its own count.
- The first time, pick the name your group will see on what you post. A first name is fine. Daniel can change it for you later.
- **Announcements** come from Daniel only. Each one is marked **Homework**, **Reminder** or **Announcement**. Homework can have a due date and a **Mark as done** tick: only you and Daniel see your tick. You can like an announcement and ask a question under it.
- **Circle**: write in the box at the top and press **Post**. The **+** button adds things to a post; for now that's **Add image** (up to four pictures).
- Pictures are made smaller on your computer before they're sent, to about 200 KB each, and what the camera wrote into the photo, such as where it was taken, is left behind. Each picture downloads to your computer once and is kept here.
- Like a post with the heart, and write a comment under it. The **•••** on your own post lets you **Edit** or **Delete my post**; on someone else's it lets you **Let Daniel know about this post** quietly, if something isn't right.
- **Share to Group Circle**: on a note, a journal entry, or under an Assistant answer, this button opens the Circle with those words already in the box. Check them before you press Post: a journal or a reading can hold things you'd rather keep private. Nothing is posted until you press Post.
- When a Quest finishes, its Group Circle stays here to read, and closes to new posts and comments.
- **Alerts**: while Sovran Advisor is open, something new shows as a normal Mac or Windows alert: a new announcement from Daniel, someone replying to your post or comment, homework due tomorrow that isn't ticked done, and Daniel having dealt with something you told him. Click an alert to open that post. The first time, your computer asks whether Sovran Advisor may show alerts; until you allow them they show at the foot of the window instead. **Settings › Alerts** has a switch for each kind; **Any new post in the Circle** starts off so it isn't noisy.
- **On your phone**: once Sovran Go is set up, it shows a **Group Circle** choice after your computer has been on. There you can read Announcements and the Circle, tick homework, like, comment and reply, and post with a photo from the phone. Nothing to type: your computer makes the phone its own Group key and passes it over sealed.
- **Alerts on your phone**: at the foot of Group Circle in Sovran Go, press **Allow alerts** and say yes when the phone asks. The same alerts then arrive on the phone, even with Sovran Go closed, with their own switches there. Tap one to open that post. On an iPhone, Sovran Go must be on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer). **Stop alerts on this phone** ends them.
- Group Circle needs your Sovran Library key (Settings › Sovran Library) and the internet. Switch it off in Settings › Modules if you'd rather not see it.

## How it works

Group Circle is the module `group` (Settings › Modules), on in a new install. Posts, comments, likes, homework ticks and pictures live in the Sovran cloud (schema `group_feed`, `cloud/group-feed.sql`), reached through the web function `group-feed` (`cloud/group-feed/index.ts`) with your Library key. The cloud finds your group from the key, never from what the app asks for, so a group can only ever read and write its own posts; Announcements are written only with Daniel's own guide key. `group-feed.js` asks with your key and passes the answer to the page; the key never reaches the page. The addresses are `GET /api/group` (who you are there and the new counts), `GET /api/group/news` and `/api/group/feed` (a page of posts), `PUT /api/group/name`, `POST /api/group/posts`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/group/posts/:id`, `POST /api/group/posts/:id/comments|like|done|flag`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/group/comments/:id`, `POST /api/group/pictures` and `GET /api/group/pictures/<group>/<name>.jpg`. Pictures are shrunk in the page (`public/group.js`: at most 1600 pixels on the long side, a JPEG of about 200 KB, drawn again so the photo's own details are left behind) and kept in `data/group-pictures` once fetched. This computer remembers only the newest post it has shown in each tab (`group.seen`), for the counts. **Share to Group Circle** leaves the words in this browser for the Circle's box (`startGroupDraft`).

The same page runs in Daniel's own app with his guide key instead (`useGroup({ keyOf: guideKey, guideApp: true })`): a group picker (or All groups), the announcement box (kind, group or all groups, a due date for homework, questions on or off), **Hide from students**, **Delete** and **Pause** on anyone's post or comment, how many marked each homework done, and two more tabs, **Flags** and **People** (`GET /api/group/flags`, `POST /api/group/flags/clear`, `GET /api/group/groups`, `POST /api/group/manage`, `POST /api/group/announcements`, `POST /api/group/posts|comments/:id/hide`). The guide key is made on that computer once (`POST /api/group/guide-key`, settings `group.guide_key`); the cloud only ever gets its fingerprint. In a student's app these addresses do nothing: the cloud refuses them without the guide key.

**Alerts** (`alerts.js`, `public/alerts.js`, `public/alerts-settings.js`): the page asks `GET /api/alerts` once a minute; the server asks the cloud what is new since it last asked (`group_feed.alerts`, `cloud/group-alerts.sql`, with your Library key) and passes on only the kinds that are switched on, each once, with the words for the alert and where it opens (`/group/<tab>#post-<id>`). This computer remembers the moment it last asked (`alerts.since`, the cloud's clock), the switches (`alerts.switches`, `GET`/`PUT /api/alerts/settings`) and which homework it has already said is due (`alerts.due_said`). The kinds: `news`, `reply`, `due`, `answer` and `feed` from the cloud, and `update` (a new version is ready, said by `public/update-nudge.js`). In Daniel's app the kinds are `report` (a new student report), `flag` and `reply` (a student commented on his post). Daniel's own note on a report never goes to the student.

**On Sovran Go** (`phone/group.js`): the phone never holds the Library key. `ensurePhoneGroup` in `phone-drop.js` makes the phone its own key (`phone_…`), registers only its fingerprint in the cloud (`group_feed.phone_key`, table `group_feed.phone_keys`, one per student, made again replaces the old one) and puts the key on the drop box's sealed shelf as the item `group` (`phone-shelf.js`), so the phone reads it with its link's own key. With Group Circle off the key is taken away. The phone calls `group-feed` straight with that key; the cloud treats it as the student, and a student switched off, or a group paused or finished, behaves the same as on the computer. Daniel can switch one phone off alone (`manage` `phone_off`). Pictures from the phone are shrunk on the phone the same way before they are sent.

On the **People** tab, a student whose phone reads Group Circle says *reads it on Sovran Go too*, and **More › Switch their phone off** takes that phone's key away alone (`manage` `phone_off`); their computer is not touched.

**Alerts on the phone** (`phone/push.js`, `phone/sw.js`, `cloud/group-push.sql`, `cloud/group-feed/push.js`): on a yes the phone subscribes with the cloud's public key (`push_key`) and keeps its push address and switches in `group_feed.push_subs` (`push_sub`, only with a phone key; Apple's, Google's, Mozilla's and Microsoft's push addresses only; it goes when the phone's key is switched off or replaced). The `group-feed` function runs `group_feed.push_run` after a post, a comment or a Tell Daniel answer, and at most every ten minutes otherwise (that is when homework due tomorrow is said, once). It keeps what is new for each phone, as its switches allow, and wakes it with an empty, signed Web Push message (VAPID, key pair made by the function the first time and kept in `group_feed.push_vapid`): no words go through the push services. Woken, the service worker fetches what waits with the phone's own key (`push_take`) and shows it; tapping opens `./?open=group&tab=…&post=…`.

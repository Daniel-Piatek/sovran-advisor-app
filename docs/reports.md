Reports is where you read your own Sovran reports: the Sovran Pattern now, later reports as they are made, and your Layered Portrait at the top once it begins.

## How you use it

- Open **Reports** in the sidebar. Each report is a card with its version, the day it was approved, its length and its sections.
- Press a card to read the report. It opens on its own ivory page, in the look of the printed report: the crown, the title and the Live Sovran wordmark at the top, a gold label on each part. **Contents** takes you to any section, and the downloads are at the top.
- **PDF download**, the small link under the title, saves the report as a white PDF, made for printing. Reading here comes first: this is where you highlight and comment.
- **Highlight and comment.** Select some words in a report: a small bar offers **Highlight** or **Comment**. Highlights show in soft gold; on a wide screen your comments sit in the white **Your comments** column beside the report, level with their words; on a phone they come together under each part. Press a highlight to change its comment or remove it (with Undo). **Your highlights** at the top lists them all.
- **Ask about this.** Select some words and press **Ask about this** (the little robot, your assistant's face) to ask your assistant about them. It reads those words with the whole report around them and, when it needs more, your own decodes in the Sovran Library. The answer comes in beside the words; press it to read it all and ask more. Your questions and answers stay there, beside the words, and also in the Assistant's list of chats, so you can carry on there. It says which part of your decodes an answer comes from only when you ask.
- Your highlights and comments are kept in this app. The report in your Sovran Library is never changed, so you always keep a clean copy.
- **On your phone** (Settings › Modules › **Phone capture**): in **Sovran Go**, choose **My reports** to read the same reports in the same look. Select words there to **Highlight**, **Comment** or **Ask my AI** (it opens Claude with the words already in the box, ready for your question; they are copied too, in case the box is empty. With ChatGPT, paste them). Highlights and comments match on both: one made on the phone arrives here within a minute of this computer being on, and one made here reaches the phone the same way. A new report, or a new version, reaches the phone once this computer has been on.
- Ask your assistant about a report ("what does my Pattern say about my gifts?"): it reads your reports too, with your highlights, comments and the questions you asked beside the words, so you can ask about what struck you.
- Your reports come from your Sovran Library, so the page needs your Library key (Settings › Sovran Library) and the internet.

## How it works

Reports is the module `reports` (Settings › Modules). The reports machine keeps each report in the Sovran Library (schema sovran_machine), locked to your own Library key; only a final report is ever given. `reports.js` asks the library link with your key (its reads list_my_reports, read_my_report and the file address) and passes the answer to the page; the key never reaches the page and nothing is kept on this computer. The addresses are `GET /api/reports`, `/api/reports/:report` and `/api/reports/:report/files/:kind` (pdf or for-your-ai); the page is `public/reports.js`; the assistant's tool is `reports_read`. Without a key, made-up reports in data/reports are read instead (only the tests use that). Highlights and comments are the table `report_marks` (`report-marks.js`, `public/report-marks.js`; `GET`/`POST /api/reports/:report/marks`, `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/report-marks/:id`, `POST /api/report-marks/:id/restore`): each keeps the chosen words with a little text before and after, so it finds its place again in a newer version of the report, and one whose words are gone is listed as no longer found. **Ask about this** is `POST /api/report-marks/:id/ask` (an answer streamed like a chat's): a highlight's first question makes its own chat (`report_marks.conversation_id`, schema 75), told the words, the section and how to look (the report first, then the decodes; no source named unless asked).

For the phone, this computer seals a copy of each report (its sections already drawn) and of your highlights with the key only it and your phone have, and puts them on a shelf in your phone's drop box (`phone-shelf.js`, `cloud/phone-drop.sql` table `shelf`). The phone holds no Library key; nobody else can read the copies. **A new link** in Settings › Phone capture closes that box and its shelf, so a lost phone reads no more. Switching Reports off empties the shelf. Highlights made on the phone come back as sealed notes (`takePhoneMark` in `report-marks.js`); when a comment was changed on both, both are kept.

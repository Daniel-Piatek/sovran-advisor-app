Private to you

Only you can see this, ever.

  • Daniel App's database on the Mac
  • Its nightly copy in your private cloud
  • Your mail, which never leaves the Mac

Shared, by key Live now

The Sovran Library. Every key opens your teaching. Each decode opens only for its own client.

  • Teaching notes and meaning pieces
  • Client decodes, one private set each

Stays with each student

Their own copy of Daniel App and everything they put in it. It lives on their computer and is never sent to you.

The order I'd do it in

  1. Reach your Mac from anywhere. Private remote access, so you can use Daniel App from your laptop or phone. Nothing moves to the cloud yet.
  2. A nightly copy to your private cloud. Done It sits in a locked area inside the library project that no student key or library tool can reach. It works as a backup, and later lets Claude reach your data when the Mac is off. Mail stays out.
  3. Daniel App connects to the library. Your copy and every student's copy can then read the teaching and decodes, each with its own key.
  4. The membership website. It's built later, on top of the library, when the courses and members are ready for it.
